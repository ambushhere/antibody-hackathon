# Thermal stability assessment

14 November 2026. This assessment defines a retrospective benchmark and a measurement fallback. The subsequent [completed ThermoMPNN-D pilot](../../results/thermal-pilot/README.md) failed to support thermal prioritisation. The protocol below records the rationale before that run. NanoMelt inference and wet lab measurements remain pending.

## Decision

- **Trastuzumab optimisation.** Start with a signed folding ΔΔG control check using ThermoMPNN-D on antigen-free paired VH/VL coordinates. Use its single substitution scores as the additive baseline and its double substitution head as a sensitivity check. Use measured scFv controls for sign and mapping, then test five published HER2 CDRH3 multi-mutants measured as IgG1. Keep current CDRH3 designs at `thermal evidence missing`. Even a successful control check would support only an exploratory folding risk flag for these clustered multi-mutants.
- **Exploratory de novo.** For bona fide VHHs, NanoMelt gives a direct sequence-based apparent Tm estimate and has released measured data and weights. Use it locally for research use consistent with its license after the external check below. For unrelated paired VH/VL designs, keep thermal evidence missing. AbMelt is the relevant paired Fv method for a later study with molecular dynamics capacity. A scaffold-relative folding score can be recorded separately for equal-length variants with a traceable parent.
- **Fallback.** Keep experimental thermal evidence missing until an antigen-free, construct-matched thermal assay provides interpretable melt curves. Use structural failures, cysteine status and sequence liabilities as separate observations. The campaign’s binding, epitope and eligibility evidence retain their own fields.

## Endpoint and organizer tools

| Quantity | Meaning in this campaign | Reporting unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Folding ΔΔG | Change in the free energy difference between the folded antibody construct and its unfolded reference state at defined conditions. With a folding free energy convention, more negative generally favours folding. An unfolding convention reverses the sign. | kcal/mol, with the tool’s sign convention |
| Binding ΔΔG | Change in free energy for association of the antibody and HER2, or another specified pair of partners. The tested Nebius `stab-ddg` takes point mutations across two partner chains and reports this endpoint. | kcal/mol, with the partner chains and sign convention |
| Apparent Tm and ΔTm | Temperature of a specified unfolding transition in a thermal ramp, and candidate Tm minus matched reference Tm. Use the same transition when comparing traces. Multi-domain constructs can show several transitions. A fixed-temperature folding ΔΔG alone cannot yield ΔTm without thermodynamic and assay information. | °C, with construct, assay and transition |

The challenge PDF (`ai-antibodies-hackathon-apheris.pdf`, pp. 2 and 8) and Apheris slides (slides 5–7) make thermostability a separate comparison with the measured trastuzumab baseline. The public participant guide suggests ThermoMPNN among stability tools. The local Foundry module inventory in [RUNBOOK.md](../../RUNBOOK.md), the pilot wrapper in [scripts/run_design_pilot.sh](../../scripts/run_design_pilot.sh) and the tested Nebius catalog (`/tmp/her2-workers/nebius-models.txt`, 34 of 59 entries) describe design, structure, interface scoring, developability, MD and binding ΔΔG tools. They provide no verified thermal prediction endpoint. [CAMPAIGN.md](../CAMPAIGN.md) and [BENCHMARK.md](../BENCHMARK.md) describe the FACS, SPR and refolding results. Their labels do not measure melting. Adaptyv’s public service lists nanoDSF and returns melt curves, Tm and aggregation data. The competition’s exact construct, ramp, comparison transition and interpretation of a VHH versus its trastuzumab baseline still need organizer confirmation. [Organizer participant guide](https://oslo-ai-antibodies-hackathon.vercel.app/participant_guide), [Adaptyv thermal assay](https://www.adaptyvbio.com/services/thermostability), [NIST nanoDSF protocol](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2023/09/25/nanoDSF_27082023.pdf).

## Tools and access

| --- | --- | --- |
| ThermoMPNN and ThermoMPNN-D | WT PDB backbone. ThermoMPNN’s custom inference selects one chain and reports scores for single substitutions. ThermoMPNN-D’s CLI accepts multiple chain IDs and reports folding ΔΔG for singles, additive doubles or joint doubles. The upstream CLI treats negative scores as stabilising. | Both repositories carry MIT licenses and public checkpoints. ThermoMPNN provides `models/thermoMPNN_default.pt` and a CPU inference path. ## Minimal paired benchmark

Use three prespecified mutant versus parent comparisons from two studies. Record the native chain and study numbering, assay, construct, replicate information and observed ΔTm. Keep measurements paired within study.

| Control | Measured apparent Tm | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morrison et al. trastuzumab scFv WT and 1.1 (`VL H91Y`) | 68.5 and 72.5 °C, ΔTm +4.0 °C | Single stabilising control. Dye-based thermal shift, two protein preparations with four technical replicates each. |
| Morrison et al. same WT and 3.2 (`VL A34D + Y49S`) | 68.5 and 62.5 °C, ΔTm −6.0 °C from Table 1 | Destabilising double control. The narrative says −5 °C. Use Table 1 for the direction check and retain the discrepancy. The author correction concerns funding. |
| Okazaki et al. trastuzumab scFv WT and `VL R66G` | 61.8 ± 0.2 and 66.7 ± 0.2 °C, ΔTm +4.9 °C | Independent stabilising single control measured with SYPRO Orange DSF under a different construct and protocol. |

Sources for the controls and format check are [Morrison Table 1 and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8514426/#Tab1), [author correction](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-27102-0), [Okazaki Table 1 and Figure 3](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.3c01948) and [Tadokoro Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1002/pro.5084). In Tadokoro’s DSC study the trastuzumab Fab melted at 82 °C and the two scFv orientations at 68 and 69 °C. These values are useful construct controls. They are unsuitable as thresholds for the hackathon assay.

For a CDRH3-adjacent stress test, Asial et al. report measured DSF Tm for a 4D5-derived anti-Grb2 scFv parent and eight selected mutants. Five contain CDRH3 changes. The parent was 68.42 °C, `W225G` 77.96 °C and `T8I + I109V + H227P` 71.39 °C. Recover the correct parent from Figure S4 and map only substitutions in the mature scored construct. Exclude signal peptide or linker mutations from a score based on paired Fv coordinates and report those constructs as unscorable for the primary comparison. This set is enriched for stabilising variants and can test their ordering and coverage. It cannot estimate false positives or stand in for HER2 trastuzumab CDRH3 data. The `W225G` mutant also lost substantial binding to Grb2. [Asial paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms3901), [Figure S4 and Table S1](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art:10.1038%2Fncomms3901/MediaObjects/41467_2013_BFncomms3901_MOESM910_ESM.pdf).

1. Derive paired Fv PDBs from 1N8Z and 4HKZ by retaining only the antibody variable domains. Use 1N8Z first and 4HKZ for sensitivity. Both structures are partner-bound snapshots, so removing the partner leaves the original conformation. I checked the public coordinates. Chain A is VL and contains A34, Y49, R66 and H91. Chain B is VH and positions 99–108 match `WGGDGFYAMD` in both sources. Before scoring, verify sequence boundaries, insertion codes, missing atoms, coordinates and source-to-model numbering. A heavy-only or light-only score cannot represent the paired Fv. [1N8Z](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1N8Z), [4HKZ](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4HKZ).
2. Pin the released checkpoint and source revision. Run the single and epistatic double modes on each paired backbone. The default 5 Å Cα cutoff includes the VL 34/49 control on both structures (4.34 Å and 4.21 Å). Set it explicitly and retain all scores using `--threshold 100`. Assign unique PDB residue numbers across both chains and save a mapping back to the source chain, residue and insertion code. Check WT letters and returned positions before joining any scores. The two principal checkpoints used by this CLI are `ThermoMPNN-ens1.ckpt` and `ThermoMPNN-D-ens1.ckpt`.

   ```bash
   python v2_ssm.py --mode single --pdb wt_fv.pdb --chains A B --threshold 100 --out fv_single
   python v2_ssm.py --mode epistatic --pdb wt_fv.pdb --chains A B --threshold 100 --distance 5.0 --out fv_double
   ```

3. Compare the direction of each measured ΔTm with the predicted folding direction. For `A34D + Y49S` report both the sum of single scores and the joint double score. Report all signed scores, coverage, sign concordance as a count and agreement across templates. A failed mapping, missing control, discordant direction or template-dependent sign leaves the affected model output unsuitable for candidate prioritisation. With three comparisons, even full concordance provides a directional sanity check. It supplies no general accuracy estimate, calibrated cutoff or conversion to °C.
4. Run the target-matched stress test below before screening eligible equal-length proposals. Report an additive folding proxy, individual substitution contributions and whether the inference is sensitive to the backbone. Inspect doubles for sensitivity to pairwise interactions without summing overlapping double scores. Leave length-changing or structurally uncertain variants unscored. Local loop interactions, conformational changes, VH/VL coupling, linker, constant domains and disulfide state limit transfer. The primary matched scFv controls change VL, and a measured panel of three or more CDRH3 substitutions in the target scFv or Fab format remains unverified.

### Target-matched CDRH3 stress test

Shanehsazzadeh et al. measured selected trastuzumab-scaffold HER2 binders after reformatting as full-length CHO-produced IgG1. The study used nanoDSF on Prometheus Panta at 0.5 mg/mL in PBS and 0.5 °C/min. I checked that the two pinned FLAb tables join on VH/VL and that their variants share the WT light chain and VH outside the annotated 13-residue CDRH3. Of the 11 variants in Supplementary Table 8, five retain that length and are usable in the fixed-backbone substitution check after mapping the study’s `SRWGGDGFYAMDY` to 1N8Z. The remaining six have length changes. Exclude FLAb’s additional candidate 12 from the minimal panel because it is absent from the primary thermal table. Retain first and later inflections separately and use the first by prespecified rule. The source narrative says all variants had lower Tm. Supplementary Table 8 instead lists candidate 4 at 80.2 °C versus 79.5 °C for WT. Treat that small difference as indeterminate for sign when repeat-level uncertainty is unavailable. [Preprint v4](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.01.08.523187v4), [author PDF with methods and Supplementary Table 8](https://absci.gcs-web.com/static-files/a53c2b92-0e77-4899-a52c-4485be691cf6), [author sequence release](https://github.com/AbSciBio/unlocking-de-novo-antibody-design/blob/cd2bd4b6932b5301d435555dc22084d407e4b92b/functionality-developability-cross_reactivity-data.csv), [FLAb first inflection CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Graylab/FLAb/e05e3f0135783ffb7de412df6a1467e7a0031911/data/thermostability/shanehsazzadeh2023unlocking_DSF1.csv), [second inflection CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Graylab/FLAb/e05e3f0135783ffb7de412df6a1467e7a0031911/data/thermostability/shanehsazzadeh2023unlocking_DSF2.csv).

## Minimal VHH benchmark

Use the two publisher-linked releases at GitLab commit `dc6a141f767aa1d913eb439b49f4df3563feca25`. I parsed both CSVs and matched uppercase, whitespace-trimmed sequences. The 640 training and 83 external rows each have internally unique sequences. Four sequence and Tm pairs occur in both files. The external IDs are `sdab33.Nb1`, `sdab35.Nb4`, `sdab36.Nb7` and `sdab442.F11`. Excluding them leaves **79** rows, including **11 nanoDSF**, 26 DSF, 30 CD and 12 DSC. The training median is **68.0 °C**. Its constant-prediction MAE is **7.76 °C** on the filtered set and **6.15 °C** on filtered nanoDSF. These are measured-label baselines calculated in this assessment. The paper’s reported external MAE of 4.8 °C is for its original cohort and has not been recomputed on these 79 rows. [Training CSV](https://gitlab.doc.ic.ac.uk/api/v4/projects/181595/repository/files/datasets%2FNanoMelt_640_nanobody_apparent_melting_temperatures.csv/raw?ref=dc6a141f767aa1d913eb439b49f4df3563feca25), [external CSV](https://gitlab.doc.ic.ac.uk/api/v4/projects/181595/repository/files/datasets%2FNanoMelt_83_external_dataset.csv/raw?ref=dc6a141f767aa1d913eb439b49f4df3563feca25), [published results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11730357/).

1. Use one ungapped mature VHH per FASTA record and freeze the ID mapping. With the released regressors and pinned embedding versions, run `nanomelt predict -i vhh_external79.fa -o vhh_external79_scores.csv -align`. Report valid coverage, MAE against the training median, Spearman rank correlation and results by assay method. Report nearest training sequence identity and group results by source and parent family where the data permit. Repeat on a similarity-filtered subset using a prespecified identity definition. The 11 nanoDSF examples are a method-matched sensitivity check with limited precision.
2. For a small within-family check on multi-mutation direction, add the independently published 76, 76H and 76G3 VHH family after checking the published sequences against both NanoMelt CSVs. The study reports 52, 58 and 65 °C by nanoDSF. This is one SARS-CoV-2 binding family with edits spanning CDR and framework, so it tests one local transfer case only. For designed HER2 VHHs, record the model’s apparent Tm and nearest training sequence identity if the check supports use. An out-of-distribution sequence or a score close to another candidate relative to observed error should remain provisional. Report VHH predictions on their own scale. [76 VHH family, Figure 2G](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2025.2571406).

## Measurement fallback and interpretation

Use purified antigen-free candidate and WT in the same batch for the optimisation comparison. Fix the exact VH/VL sequence context, linker and orientation for scFv, and constant domains for a full Fab if present. Match tags, buffer, pH, concentration, redox and ramp. For a de novo VHH, use a known VHH as a same-format assay control and the organizer’s specified trastuzumab reference for any contest comparison. Prespecify how to compare multiple transitions and calculate ΔTm with replicate variability. Record aggregation and onset alongside Tm. Report weak intrinsic signal, ambiguous transitions, aggregation-dominated traces and expression failures explicitly as missing or uninterpretable thermal measurements. CDR substitutions can change the Trp/Tyr fluorescence signal. DSC can provide an orthogonal transition measurement if sufficient material is available and the method is feasible. A matched measurement can establish the observed shift under its assay conditions. Binding, competition, shelf life and performance under a different expression or formulation protocol require their own evidence. [Adaptyv thermal assay](https://www.adaptyvbio.com/services/thermostability), [NIST nanoDSF protocol](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2023/09/25/nanoDSF_27082023.pdf), [Tadokoro DSC comparison](https://doi.org/10.1002/pro.5084).
