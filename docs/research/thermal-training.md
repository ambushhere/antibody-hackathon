# Measured thermal data for paired trastuzumab designs

14 November 2026. **The pinned FLAb release does not support a thermal ranking for campaign designs.** It contains 19 scFv observations from two families in one study and 37 measurements on purified Fabs from five lineages in another. Holding out either study leaves zero rows in its format and assay stratum. A family-only split can probe transfer inside the study protocol. The held-out HER2 CDRH3 panel was measured as full-length IgG1. I skipped the ridge and training median. The earlier ThermoMPNN-D pilot failed on measured controls and CDRH3 ordering. Its folding ΔΔG estimates supply no Tm labels. [Thermal proxy pilot](../../results/thermal-pilot/README.md).

## Data and exclusions

Audited 21 CSVs at Graylab/FLAb commit `e05e3f0135783ffb7de412df6a1467e7a0031911`. The actual 1,324 records include 619 paired VH/VL rows and 705 single-domain rows. Of the paired rows, 606 report an apparent Tm or inflection, including 86 with unverified units or measured construct, and 13 report DLS polydispersity. Counts use parsed CSV records. The pinned README lists 673 NbThermo records, while the pinned CSV contains 672. The audit reads the named measurement columns and strips a trailing U+200B from affected values. It checks canonical amino acids and rejects two NbThermo sequence rows containing `O` and an internal space, respectively. These are syntax checks on variable-domain strings. The original sequence boundaries and physical constructs come from the study methods. [Pinned FLAb tables](https://github.com/Graylab/FLAb/tree/e05e3f0135783ffb7de412df6a1467e7a0031911/data/thermostability), [FLAb2 paper](https://doi.org/10.64898/2025.12.27.696706).

## Lineage, duplicates and uncertainty

- The audit quarantines all 39 Shanehsazzadeh observations and the one Jain 2017 WT measurement. It also screens for the names trastuzumab, Herceptin and 4D5 in available lineage fields, exact WT VL, ≥95% VH similarity or ≥90% on both chains. No additional FLAb row met the sequence flags. These flags help detect near-WT rows. Distant derived lineages can evade a sequence cutoff, especially after CDR grafting, and the anonymous tables lack lineage annotations. A future fit should resolve source IDs and remove all known 4D5 descendants, including engineered binders for other antigens. The Morrison, Okazaki and Asial studies supply held-out controls only. [Fab4D5 library example](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC515084/), [Morrison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8514426/), [Okazaki](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.3c01948), [Asial](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms3901).
- Four exact paired sequence keys recur across sources, one of them the trastuzumab WT. The adalimumab key has 71.0 °C in Jain 2017 and 57.9 °C in Rosace. Golimumab has 70.0 and 62.4 °C, respectively. These comparisons quantify label shifts across construct and assay and supply no estimate of measurement error. The audit lists all duplicates, collapses repeated identical values within source, format, assay and endpoint, and excludes conflicting repeats from the unique-label summary. A second count drops shared exact keys from all non-target paired strata. That conservative accounting leaves 17 Rosace scFvs and 37 Hie Fabs. It does not resolve near-duplicate families. [Duplicate inventory](../../results/thermal-training/duplicates.json).
- The 13 sequence pairs in the FLAb target panel match the Absci author release at `cd2bd4b6932b5301d435555dc22084d407e4b92b` by WT scaffold, VH loop and values. That release includes 12 mutants. The primary Supplementary Table 8 lists 11, so case 12 stays an author-release observation outside the primary comparison. The panel was selected for binding and other properties. The WT has 79.52 °C for both reported inflections. Among IDs 1–11, first inflection shifts range from −9.54 to +0.72 °C and second inflection shifts from −9.13 to +3.21 °C. Three variants have a lower first inflection and a higher second inflection than WT. Repeat-level uncertainty and trace quality are unavailable in these tables. [Author measurement CSV](https://github.com/AbSciBio/unlocking-de-novo-antibody-design/blob/cd2bd4b6932b5301d435555dc22084d407e4b92b/functionality-developability-cross_reactivity-data.csv), [primary supplement PDF](https://absci.gcs-web.com/static-files/a53c2b92-0e77-4899-a52c-4485be691cf6).
- Nearest training similarity is undefined with zero training rows. For the 12 primary-table pairs including WT, nearest non-target similarity to observed Rosace scFvs is 77.6–78.9% and to observed Hie Fabs 77.0–78.4%. WT alone is 78.1% and 77.5%, respectively. Similarity is one minus summed VH and VL Levenshtein edits divided by the sum of each chain's maximum pairwise length. This is a sequence coverage diagnostic. Accuracy within either protocol, cross-source error and candidate-level confidence intervals remain unestimated. With a single target scaffold, resampling variants would give no estimate of between-family uncertainty. [Audit output](../../results/thermal-training/audit.json).

## Next measurement

For the current decision, measure antigen-free WT, candidate CDRH3 variants and an assay control side by side in the actual submission format. Match linker and orientation or Fab constant domains, expression and purification conditions, buffer, concentration and ramp. Prespecify the comparable inflection, track aggregation and retain raw curves. Use at least two independent preparations and repeat scans so the observed ΔTm can be compared with WT variability. Treat aggregation-dominated or weak fluorescence traces as uninterpretable. Test unlabeled synthetic mutations as a deliberately diverse panel of singles and combinations with comparable mutation counts. Keep a blinded measured tranche for ranking evaluation and measure HER2 binding and competition separately. A model trained with trastuzumab labels would answer a separate within-scaffold question. [Adaptyv nanoDSF](https://www.adaptyvbio.com/services/thermostability), [Tadokoro format comparison](https://doi.org/10.1002/pro.5084).

For a future transfer benchmark, Rosace's original supplement can expand the nanoDSF scFv set with CR3022 and replicate information under the same source protocol. The public Tadokoro/Brevity workbook is a separate route for dye DSF. I verified 904 populated measurement rows, 188 unique paired sequences and 97 WT rows in `PRO-33-e5029-s002.xlsx`. It is one nivolumab family, and the paper's 186 passing mutants use yield QC. Recover the QC mapping and median measured Tm per sequence before considering that panel. Keep it in a dye DSF stratum. Use parent-matched ΔTm and protocol-compatible related data from additional sources. Evaluate source holdout with related families and duplicate pairs purged from each training fold. Inside each fold compare a fixed, standardized sequence-feature ridge against the training median using MAE and within-family rank correlation, then report the range across sources and a source/family-level interval if enough independent groups exist. Reserve every target lineage and use an independent format-matched CDRH3 panel for the final ranking test. [Rosace supplementary methods](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-023-37668-6/MediaObjects/41467_2023_37668_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), [Tadokoro/Brevity paper and supplement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11129621/#sec18).

## Replay

Use local paths for the pinned FLAb `data/thermostability/` directory and the Absci author CSV. The audit expects the 21 pinned table names, checks the required CSV columns, refuses malformed CSV rows, and checks the author join. It reads UTF-8 CSV up to 2 MB per file and emits finite numeric values or JSON null. It uses Python 3.13.18 and RapidFuzz 3.14.6 from the repository environment. The report records seed 0 and uses no random operations. [FLAb source tree](https://github.com/Graylab/FLAb/tree/e05e3f0135783ffb7de412df6a1467e7a0031911/data/thermostability), [Absci source CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AbSciBio/unlocking-de-novo-antibody-design/cd2bd4b6932b5301d435555dc22084d407e4b92b/functionality-developability-cross_reactivity-data.csv).

```bash
source .venv/bin/activate
export PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1
python scripts/benchmark_thermal_training.py \
  --flab-dir "$FLAB_THERMOSTABILITY_DIR" \
  --absci-csv "$ABSCI_AUTHOR_CSV" \
  --output results/thermal-training
python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider tests/test_thermal_training.py
```
