## Cross-experiment reading rules

- The 75 distant SPR variants are nested within the 158 primary variants. Pairwise, AbLang2, neural and blend rows reuse those measured labels and held-out predictions; they are method comparisons, not additional validation cohorts.
- The external panel is a separate readout, but its 25 numeric KDs are for reported binders. Its 11 reported negatives have missing KD, which cannot be imputed as a negative affinity value.
- Structural sample totals count poses. The 21 OpenDDE poses are seven sequences by three seeds; the 72 template-control poses reuse four sequences across templates, seeds and draws; the context panel also reuses seven sequences across target contexts. These counts must not be summed as independent molecules.
- FACS AP, SPR rank correlation, contact recovery, ipTM, folding ΔΔG and apparent Tm are different endpoints. No result establishes a universal best model or a weighted score across endpoints.
- No newly designed candidate has measured HER2 affinity, trastuzumab competition/epitope preservation or Tm improvement. Predicted or locally screened status is not a measured gain or competition-ready designation.
