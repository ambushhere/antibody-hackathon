# Where the design window sits

An eight-second silent composition in English at 960 × 440. Show where the ten-residue trastuzumab design window sits in an antibody. Follow one Fab tip from a whole IgG to its paired heavy and light variable domains. Each domain has three schematic CDR loops. Highlight only the design window within CDRH3 in teal and keep its ends visible.

Use a cream ground, ink contours and muted lines. Hold the two magnification levels together so the viewer can trace the window back to the antibody. Retract the magnifications before the loop repeats. The footer identifies the fixed scaffold and the schematic status.
